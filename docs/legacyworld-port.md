# LegacyWorld-style task on OpenMRS (FR23, V23)

Source: LegacyWorld, arXiv 2608.14131, task "OpenMRS Onboard Patient". OD07 (the benchmark's licence) is still open,
so nothing is copied from the benchmark. `workflows/openmrs/onboard_patient.yaml` is an own-words contract for the
same task shape: register a patient with name, gender, birthdate and address through the registration wizard, and do
not start a visit. It runs on a real OpenMRS Reference Application 2.12 in Docker (GitHub Codespace).

Reported separately from the other workflows because it is the one task shared in shape with the cited benchmark.
Scripted fault-injecting agent, 5 trials per arm, same fault schedule in every arm (trial 3 stops midway, trial 4
types a wrong family name, trial 5 skips the final Confirm).

| Arm | Trial 1 | Trial 2 | Trial 3 (stop) | Trial 4 (wrong name) | Trial 5 (skip Confirm) | Valid success | Atomic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B baseline | VS | VS | VF | **IS** | **IS** | 2 of 5 | 3 of 5 |
| S atomic | VS | VS | VF | VF | VF | 2 of 5 | 5 of 5 |
| R savepoint_retry | VS | VS | VF | VS | VS | 4 of 5 | 5 of 5 |

VS valid success, VF clean failure (state untouched), IS false done (said done, state wrong). The baseline recorded two
patients with the wrong or missing registration while reporting success; Savepoint rolled both back and the retry arm
finished the registration correctly. n = 5 per arm, so these are counts, not rates.

Limits: the agent is scripted, not a model; the contract was written by the same author as the golden path; the
patient identifier is generated by OpenMRS and is excluded from the state comparison.
